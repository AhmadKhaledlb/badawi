# BADAWI Product Specification

This document summarizes the locked BADAWI product decisions that implementation must preserve.

## Product Purpose

BADAWI is a real-world cultural learning and field-capability product focused on environmental understanding, practical competence, reflection, and safe real-world transfer.

It is not a tourism guide, entertainment app, conventional gamified learning app, or passive content library.

## V1 Audience

Primary:
- 18–35 adventure travelers
- solo travelers and backpackers
- beginners seeking structured real-world capability

Secondary:
- scouts
- travel creators
- students and learners interested in field competence

## Product Hierarchy

Region → Environment → Pack → Unit → Challenge

## V1 Scope

- Region: Arabian Peninsula
- Environment: Desert
- Pack: Desert Foundations
- Units: 6
- Challenges: 28

## Core Product Principles

- Real-world learning over passive consumption
- Competence over engagement metrics
- Reflection and transfer are part of the learning experience
- Safety is integrated into progression
- Stopping, modifying, postponing, or refusing can demonstrate competence
- Difficulty increases through independence and integration, not danger
- No conventional XP/leaderboard/streak-pressure model unless explicitly approved
- No social/community layer in V1 unless separately approved
- Cross-feature additions must remain within locked V1 scope

## Core Experience Areas

Implementation must preserve the approved Phase 1 product flows, including:

- onboarding
- home/navigation
- region/environment/pack exploration
- unit/challenge progression
- challenge preparation
- Field Mode
- post-attempt review
- achievements/profile
- safety acceptance
- progress and state continuity

## Scope Discipline

Claude must not introduce unapproved features or expand V1 merely because they are common in other learning, travel, fitness, or adventure products.

When product behavior is ambiguous and the ambiguity could materially change user experience, progression, safety, scope, or learning meaning, escalate rather than inventing behavior.
