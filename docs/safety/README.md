# BADAWI Safety, Research & Cultural Governance

This document summarizes the locked BADAWI safety, research-verification, and cultural-governance requirements that implementation must preserve.

## Governing Principle

BADAWI may support real-world field activity.

Safety, factual reliability, research integrity, and cultural authenticity therefore take precedence over completion speed, engagement, aesthetics, and technical convenience.

Claude may implement systems that deliver BADAWI knowledge. Claude's own model knowledge is not evidence that field guidance is safe, factual, verified, or culturally authentic.

## Safety Doctrine

BADAWI challenge difficulty increases through:

- independence
- integration
- judgment
- adaptation

It must not increase through greater danger.

Stopping, modifying, postponing, or refusing a challenge may demonstrate competence.

The product must never reward unsafe persistence or pressure a learner to continue when conditions, preparation, equipment, knowledge, or personal judgment indicate that stopping is appropriate.

## Safety-Critical Content

Claude must not independently create authoritative production guidance concerning areas such as:

- hydration
- heat exposure
- navigation
- shelter
- weather hazards
- wildlife
- emergency response
- environmental hazards
- edible or unsafe materials
- survival procedures

Use approved content or clearly identified development placeholders.

A disclaimer does not convert unsafe or unverified guidance into acceptable guidance.

## Research Integrity

Never fabricate:

- citations
- books
- studies
- experts
- quotations
- sources
- provenance
- verification results

If a claim cannot be verified, it remains unverified.

If credible sources materially disagree, preserve the disagreement and escalate rather than manufacturing certainty.

## Content Trust

Where applicable, content must preserve its relevant:

- source/provenance
- verification state
- review state
- version
- uncertainty
- safety status
- cultural review status

Claude may format, structure, validate, and implement content, but may not promote content to a higher trust state merely because Claude generated or processed it.

Engineering completion does not imply content approval.

## Traditional & Cultural Knowledge

Do not treat the Arabian Peninsula, Bedouin communities, tribes, countries, regions, environments, or historical periods as culturally homogeneous.

Avoid unsupported generalizations such as presenting a locally or historically specific practice as something all Bedouin or Arabian communities do.

Claude must not independently determine:

- that a practice is authentically traditional
- which tribe or community owns or uses a practice
- that a modern practice represents historical practice
- that different traditions can be merged
- that a cultural claim is universally applicable

Match specificity to evidence.

When attribution is uncertain, preserve uncertainty.

## Traditional Knowledge vs Modern Safety

Traditional or historically documented knowledge is not automatically recommended modern field guidance.

Where traditional practice and credible contemporary safety guidance differ, preserve the distinction and escalate where necessary.

Cultural authenticity must never be used to justify unsafe field instruction.

## Cultural Representation

Do not introduce fabricated or stereotypical cultural material merely to create atmosphere.

Avoid unsupported:

- tribal motifs
- pseudo-historical sayings
- invented folklore
- invented Arabic expressions
- imaginary customs
- culturally synthetic decoration presented as authentic

Represent communities as living, contemporary, diverse communities rather than as primitive, mystical, exotic, uniformly nomadic, or frozen in history.

## Safety Gates

Approved warnings, acknowledgements, prerequisites, stop conditions, unavailable states, and safety checks must not be weakened or removed for convenience or engagement.

Safety-critical information must remain appropriately accessible and must not depend solely on transient UI when persistent access is required.

Where Phase 4 requires offline access to safety information, implementation must preserve that guarantee.

## Field Mode

Field Mode must fail safely.

Network loss, missing content, corrupted state, unavailable device capability, or unexpected application failure must not cause BADAWI to fabricate guidance or falsely imply that required information is available.

Prefer safe degradation over false continuity.

## AI-Assisted Content

AI may assist future content-production workflows only within the approved research and review process.

AI-generated substantive field, safety, historical, or cultural content is not automatically verified production content.

Model confidence is not domain verification.

## Independent Readiness Dimensions

Where applicable, distinguish:

1. Engineering readiness
2. Content/research readiness
3. Safety/cultural approval

A feature may be technically complete while remaining blocked from production because another required readiness dimension is incomplete.

## Escalation

Stop and escalate when implementation encounters:

- missing safety-critical information
- conflicting safety guidance
- uncertain cultural attribution
- unsupported substantive claims
- unresolved source disagreement
- pressure to weaken a safety control
- a conflict between traditional practice and modern safety guidance
- any requirement that would reward or encourage unnecessary physical risk

Do not fill these gaps with plausible invention.
